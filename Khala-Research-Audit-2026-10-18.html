Slide 15: the IP claim is real, but narrower than the previous wording

Links IPs to wallet pseudonyms/addresses from traffic timing under a strong passive observer. Researchers used controlled endpoints and emulated routers, not an actual ISP deployment. Three or four observations and frequency-filter assumptions matter. Not population-wide exposure or named-person identification.

Dynamic IPs, NAT, missing observations and network conditions can reduce success. Tor/VPN defenses are not resolved by the study; UR (SN25) was not tested. This is a preprint result, not an independent replication.

Exact reported results: Ethereum mainnet/MetaMask 95.33%; Ethereum testnet/MetaMask 96.80%; Bitcoin testnet/Electrum 97.70%; Solana testnet/Torus 96.58%. The website rounds these to one decimal.

Primary paper: Wang et al., arXiv:2508.21440v1, §§8–10. This audit checks the authors’ reported result; it does not independently replicate it.
