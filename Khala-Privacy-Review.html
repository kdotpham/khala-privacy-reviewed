Khala privacy deck: final-pass review

Scope: the latest 23 retained slides, numbered in display order. These correspond to original PDF pages 3–25. Layout review uses the supplied rendered PDF and the current web export. Colours were checked against the navy/slate web styles. The appendix remains excluded.

The table records defects in the original reviewed version. The separate revision applies the proposed presentation changes. This is a formatting and clarity review, not an independent verification of every protocol, market or legal claim. Detailed source notes remain outside the slides as requested.

Highest-impact changes

  1. Remove the sample growth history and publish only the supplied user snapshot.
  2. Separate adoption definitions and keep conditions and experimental scope beside the claims.
  3. Shorten left-column titles to two lines and raise small reading text.
  4. Restore continuous page numbers and consistent snapshot metadata.
  5. Use one unit for scenario comparisons and distinguish research programmes from live products.

Overall verdict on the original: return for rework.
